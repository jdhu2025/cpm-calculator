## Verified product facts

- CPM mode: ad spend divided by impressions, multiplied by 1,000.
- Budget mode: target CPM multiplied by impressions, divided by 1,000.
- Impressions mode: budget divided by target CPM, multiplied by 1,000.
- Reverse CPM mode: budget divided by target impressions, multiplied by 1,000.
- Campaign planner inputs include budget, target impressions, flight length, region, objective, industry, platforms, priorities, and ad format.
